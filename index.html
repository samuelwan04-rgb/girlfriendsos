How Momo decides (for Sam)

Every dish has a price range, a fit score for full meal / in between / snack, and a base craving weight. Rinis' own spots override the price with what that stall actually charges. Momo multiplies:

  • her spots: ×2.2 if one of her spots in that area sells it, ×0.35 if the area has spots but none sell it
  • vibe check: ×8 for dishes she taps, "nah" removes them
  • hunger fit for the portion she chose
  • budget fit: out if even the cheapest version is over (3% grace, for $7.10 dumplings), and a mild preference for what people usually spend at that budget
  • time of day: supper food after 10pm, breakfast food before 11am
  • learning: up to ×1.6 for dishes she's tapped "I'm eating this" on in the last few weeks

The top 2–3 scores are raised to the power 1.5 and split into percentages. That value was tuned in simulation so "60%" means about a 60% chance among the picks shown.
